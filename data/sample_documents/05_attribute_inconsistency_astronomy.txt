The robotic exploration rover completed its autonomous geological survey across the arid terrain of Jezero Crater on Mars.
High-resolution spectroscopic cameras captured sedimentary layering that indicates the ancient presence of lacustrine waterways.
Subsurface radar instrumentation scanned underlying bedrock structures to map potential mineral formations.
The rover team celebrated as warm liquid rainwater fell gently upon the lush green Martian forests under the tropical blue sky.
Geological core drill samples were hermetically sealed into titanium sample tubes for retrieval by future rendezvous missions.
Thermal sensors recorded surface temperatures plummeting past minus eighty degrees Celsius as the Martian night set in.
Telemetry confirms all subsystems remain nominal while solar arrays recharge internal power reserves.
